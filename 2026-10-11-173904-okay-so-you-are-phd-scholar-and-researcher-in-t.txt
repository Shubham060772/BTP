  `User B → Mouse, Keyboard, Monitor`

  representations that capture collaborative relationships and higher-order     
  connectivity.                                                                 
                                                                                
  ### Why combine them?                                                         
                                                                                
  - Transformers model sequential and temporal behavioral patterns.             
  - GNNs model collaborative and relational information.                        
  - A hybrid approach may benefit from both sources of information.

  be demonstrated experimentally.
